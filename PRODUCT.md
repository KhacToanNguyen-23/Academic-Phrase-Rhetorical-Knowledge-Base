# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Academic curators, linguists, researchers, and academic writers.
- Curators/reviewers review extracted rhetorical phrase frames mined from academic papers, verify slot masks and epistemic claim strengths, and accept or reject candidates for the knowledge base.
- Writers compose scientific manuscripts with rhetorical guidance and citation/evidence grounding.

## Product Purpose

Academic Phrase & Rhetorical Knowledge Base (AP-RKB) provides a rigorous, traceable repository of rhetorical sentence frames mined from academic literature. It enables fast, accurate triage and curation of extracted phrases with provenance, source context, and journal attribution.

## Brand Personality

- Tone: Scholarly, precise, restrained, trustworthy.
- Key adjectives: Academic, focused, ergonomic.
- Emotional goals: Clarity and speed during bulk curation; deep confidence in academic rigor.

## Anti-references

- Overly decorated SaaS dashboards with neon gradients or giant floating shadows.
- Cluttered, misaligned form controls and broken wrapping.
- Hidden or awkward triage buttons that slow down high-volume review workflows.

## Design Principles

- Data-first ergonomics: Reviewers triage hundreds of phrases; affordances must be immediate, legible, and frictionless.
- Traceable provenance: Always display the source sentence with highlighted slots, article title, and journal metadata.
- Clear semantic hierarchy: The canonical frame is the hero; slots, badges, and actions support without visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance with minimum 4.5:1 text contrast.
- Full keyboard operability and visible focus rings.
- Semantic HTML tags and clear ARIA labels for checkboxes and action buttons.
