# Landing report (Phase 04)

Scope: desktop web only (1440px; owner decision 2026-11-04). Route `/`, lazy chunk `LandingPage` (about 27 kB JS, 2 kB CSS).

## Direction

`sentence-as-layout`, seed 568972173, drawn 2026-11-03. No redraw of the direction. Type and palette were replaced by the owner's brand (Be Vietnam Pro + Newsreader, paper / ink / one teal) as recorded in `plan.md`; the draw run itself redrew palette and type twice by mistake, see the warning at the top of `taste/landing-sheet.md`.

## Idea and live thing

- Idea: on this page, the slot of a phrase frame becomes the media slot inside the headline. The headline reads "The right [suggests that] for every claim" and the bracketed slot shows a real phrase instead of saying the word "phrase".
- Live thing: a `ToggleGroup` of four rhetorical moves (Gap, Method, Finding, Limitation). Choosing one swaps the slot phrase (240 ms, opacity and transform) and shows the full frame plus its claim strength. Keyboard works; the group never goes empty.
- Colour by chapter: the fixed header takes the ink and ground of the chapter under it (300 ms).
- Signature on the first screen: one headline in two copies clipped at the seam, so the ink changes inside the glyphs of line two.

## Verification (production build, `--widths 1440`)

- `lint.mjs`: 0 FAIL, 0 WARN. `inspect.mjs`: 0 FAIL, 2 WARN.
- WARN `mixed-font-headline`: the slot is set in the phrase face (Newsreader) inside a Be Vietnam Pro headline. It is the product's own live specimen and the one place the idea shows; it is not decoration.
- WARN `size-count` (11 sizes): fluid display steps (hero, section words, verbs, wordmark) plus five text sizes. Defended as one display scale; the five text sizes are 14, 16, 17, 20, 22.
- Browser-level checks by script (no Playwright): toggle swaps slot and caption for all four moves; unselecting keeps one selected; anchors `#how`, `#start`, `#questions` exist; open FAQ row takes the tile tone; no horizontal overflow at 1024px; one `h1`; `<html lang>` follows the copy locale.
- `dist/` has no `dev/ui` or `demo-session`.

## Critique (last round, `references/critique.md` shape, short)

Strong: the first screen answers "what is this" in the headline and sentence, the seam through line two, the how-it-works panels with one verb each, the cropped wordmark.
Weak: (1) the 4:5 plate in the intro leaves empty ground under it; (2) the list section is quiet next to the panels above it; (3) FAQ answers are only seen once a row is opened. Not scored numerically; owner review needed.

## Deviations from the Phase 04 spec

- No `motion` package: pnpm's `minimumReleaseAge` policy rejects the current lockfile when adding it (Tailwind 4.3.4 is inside the window), and CSS transitions plus a small IntersectionObserver cover every move used. Entrances are staggered 80 ms, travel is transform and opacity only, and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the travel.
- Frames are written by hand in `frames.ts`, not generated by `gen:landing-frames`: the backend seed has only gap and finding intents, and method and limitation frames had to be self-authored anyway.
- The no-JS fallback cannot exist: the app is a single-page React app, so without script nothing renders. The `<details>` FAQ still works without any extra script once the page is up.
- Primary actions link to `/signin?next=/phrasebank` for guests, because the phrasebank is a staff screen.
- The "list" table links go to staff screens through the route guard (guests land on sign in with `next`).
